# REVIEW.md

Defects in the starter `app/main.py`, and what replaced them.

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `health` / 72 | Always returns ok. Never pings MongoDB. | Stop the database: the route still returns 200. The runner treats that as ready. | Ping with `admin.command("ping")`. Failure is 503. |
| 2 | module startup | No indexes. Uniqueness is check-then-insert. | Two identical creates or punch-ins at the same moment can both return 201. | Unique `emp_code`, unique `(emp_code, date)`, plus the query indexes, created in the app lifespan. Duplicate key becomes 409. |
| 3 | `list_employees` / 92 | `skip = page * page_size`, so page 1 drops the first page. | `GET /employees?page=1` starts at employee 21. | Skip `(page - 1) * page_size`. |
| 4 | `list_employees` / 94 | `total` counts the whole collection, and rows are not ordered. | `?department=Engineering` reports total 6 when two people match. Order is undefined. | `count_documents` on the same filter. Sort `emp_code` ascending. `page_size` capped at 100. |
| 5 | `create_employee` / 77 | No schema checks. `created_at` is a naive local `datetime`, returned as a datetime. | `emp_code: "NOPE"`, equal shift times, or a bad email return 201. `created_at` is not epoch milliseconds. | Pydantic patterns and a shift-times validator. Store a UTC datetime truncated to a second. Return epoch milliseconds. |
| 6 | `punch_in` / 101 | Unknown `emp_code` crashes on `emp["shift_start"]`. Any status string is stored. The response includes MongoDB `id`. | Missing employee is 500. `status: "ABSENT"` is stored. Clients see `_id`. | 404 when the employee is missing. Status limited to PRESENT, WFH, ON_DUTY. Response built without `_id`. |
| 7 | `compute_late_minutes` / 33 | Late only when elapsed whole minutes are greater than 10. 09:40:01 is 10 minutes and was stored as 0. No IST, no second truncation, no overnight date. | Shift 09:30, punch 09:40:01 returns `late_minutes: 0`. A 05:30 IST punch on a 22:00–06:00 shift lands on the wrong date. | R1 and R2: truncate to a second, IST date with the overnight rule, late only after a strict 10:00, counted from shift start. |
| 8 | `compute_work_hours` / 40 and `compute_overtime` / 44 | `round()` is banker's rounding. Overtime has no 30-minute floor. Overnight shift end stays on the attendance date. | 3618 seconds (1.005 hours) can round to 1.00. Five minutes past shift end is stored as overtime. A 06:40 IST punch-out on a night shift gets 0. | Decimal half-up for hours. Overtime only when the floored minutes are at least 30. Shift end of an overnight shift is the next IST day. |
| 9 | `list_attendance` / 144 | Loads every matching document, sorts in Python by date only, and returns `id`. Missing `history` / `half_day` are omitted. | On ~100k rows the route reads the collection into memory. Two people on the same date are not ordered by `emp_code`. The legacy EMP0002 row has no `half_day` or `history`. | Filter, sort (`date` desc, `emp_code` asc), skip, and limit in MongoDB, hinted to an index. `date_from > date_to` is 422. Serializer fills the missing defaults and drops `_id`. |
| 10 | bottom TODO / 153 | Punch-out, regularization, four analytics routes, and explain are absent. | Those paths are 404. | Implemented against R1–R10: atomic punch-out, history append with a compare-and-set, aggregations for the analytics, `$densify` for the daily trend, and `explain` with `executionStats` on the same pipelines. |

Looked at and left as not a defect: `load_dotenv()` does not override variables that are already set, so a real `MONGO_URI` wins. The attendance list's page formula was already `(page - 1) * page_size`. The sample documents match `DATA_MODEL.md`; the bugs were in how the starter read and wrote them, not in the samples.
